close, and it is not the place to check what is still open. Nothing should be

Enhancement / clarity: When solving from "items I have" (the owned-inventory / Trove-restricted pool), make it clear that the augments shown in the slots are NOT necessarily augments I already own — they are recommendations. The owned-inventory pool constrains the base items to what I have, but augments (and other crafting) still come from the full catalog, so an augment appearing in a slot is "what you should craft/slot here," not "one of yours." The results should say this plainly so users don't assume the suggested augments are already in their possession.

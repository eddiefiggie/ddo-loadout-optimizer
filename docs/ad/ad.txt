 DDO LOADOUT OPTIMIZER — your best-in-slot gear, proven by math
                  (free, in-browser, no login)
================================================================

  >>  Try it: https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/

Tired of juggling six wiki tabs and a spreadsheet to work out which
named items give you the most of a stat WITHOUT wasting a bonus type
you already have covered?

This isn't a tier list, and it isn't "what a good player usually
wears." It's a real OPTIMIZER: you tell it your build and rank the
stats you care about, and it searches every wiki-sourced named item,
augment, set bonus, and crafting option (9,000+ indexed) and hands
back the SINGLE loadout that is mathematically the best for your
priorities — slot by slot, with the receipts.

No install. No login. Nothing to download. Free. Runs entirely in
your browser, on desktop or mobile.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 1.  TELL IT HOW YOU ACTUALLY PLAY
----------------------------------------------------------------
Set your level, race, and armor — then your combat style, down to
weapon type and even a dual-wield off-hand weapon, a crossbow + rune
arm, or a druid's "no metal" oath. It only considers gear you can
truly equip, so nothing is wasted.

   [ SCREENSHOT -> https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/screenshots/ad/1-character.png ]


----------------------------------------------------------------
 2.  RANK WHAT MATTERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Add the stats you care about and order them. #1 is maximized first,
then #2 without giving up any of #1, and so on — that ordering IS the
objective the solver optimizes. One-click bundles get you started.

   [ SCREENSHOT -> https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/screenshots/ad/2-priorities.png ]


----------------------------------------------------------------
 3.  GET THE PROVABLY-OPTIMAL LOADOUT
----------------------------------------------------------------
In under a second you get the single best loadout for your
priorities — every slot, plus the EXACT crafting steps (augments,
seals, set pieces) needed to build it. Not a guess. Provably optimal.

   [ SCREENSHOT -> https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/screenshots/ad/3-loadout.png ]


----------------------------------------------------------------
 4.  IT SHOWS ITS WORK
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every point of every stat is traced to the exact item and bonus
type. No hand-waving — you can see precisely where your Constitution
37 (or your Doublestrike, or your Physical Sheltering) comes from.

   [ SCREENSHOT -> https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/screenshots/ad/4-proof.png ]


----------------------------------------------------------------
 5.  EXPLORE THE TRADE-OFFS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Near-optimal alternatives show exactly what you'd gain and give up
to complete a different set, free up a slot, or take fewer crafting
steps. Click any one to inspect it against the optimum.

   [ SCREENSHOT -> https://eddiefiggie.github.io/ddo-loadout-optimizer/screenshots/ad/5-alternatives.png ]
